How I got here

Different kitchens. Same care for the craft.

Software was a career change, but not a reset. I carried forward the habits I learned in demanding kitchens: prepare well, communicate clearly, and make the whole team better.

2009 — 2017

Learning the craft of a team

I worked in fine-dining kitchens in New York and Denver, including Michelin-caliber restaurants. Owning a station taught me to stay calm under pressure, notice the details, and take responsibility for the whole service.

2012

Studying media and people

I earned a B.A. in Media Studies from Vassar College. That interest in how people understand and use things still shapes how I approach product engineering.

2017 — 2018

Making the move into software

At Turing School of Software & Design, I learned software engineering by building and shipping projects with a team. Those early builds were the start of a new craft, not the work I do today.

2017 — 2020

First production systems

At Fifth Third Bank, I joined the Dobot team, revived a Rails backend, improved delivery pipelines, and built Python services for authentication. It was my first chance to apply engineering practices to a real financial product.

2020 — Today

Growing into technical leadership

At Housecall Pro, I’ve grown from software engineer to staff engineer and taken on engineering management along the way. Today I work across fintech architecture, product delivery, and the systems that help teams move safely at scale.
