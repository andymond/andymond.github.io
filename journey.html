How I got here

I started in service work, then moved into kitchens.

In 2017, I started studying software engineering at Turing while I was still working in kitchens. I carried over the parts that mattered: preparation, clear handoffs, and staying calm when the plan changes.

2009 — 2018

Service work, then kitchens

I started working in the service industry in 2009, including front-of-house and other jobs. From 2014 to 2018, I cooked in fine-dining kitchens in New York and Denver, including Michelin-caliber restaurants. On the line, preparation and clear handoffs mattered every night.

2012

Vassar College

I earned a B.A. in Media Studies. I was interested in how people make sense of information, a question that still comes up in product engineering.

2017 — 2018

Learning to write software

At Turing School of Software & Design, I learned Ruby, Rails, and the habits of building software with a team. I was learning the basics; the projects below show that stage of my work.

2017 — 2020

Fifth Third Bank

I joined the Dobot team, revived a Rails backend, maintained CI/CD pipelines, and built Python services for authentication. It was my first software job and a good introduction to keeping a financial product running.

2020 — Today

Housecall Pro

I moved from software engineer to staff engineer and managed a squad from 2023–2025. Today I partner with the risk squad on architecture and review work across payments and invoices.
